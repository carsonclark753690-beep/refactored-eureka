Questions

Things you might
be wondering.

When does Atlas enter service?

Atlas is currently in flight-test phase. We're targeting commercial entry into service in late 2028, with our first commercial corridor connecting San Francisco and London.

Is flying at 60,000 feet safe?

The stratosphere is in many ways the safest layer of the atmosphere — there's no weather, no other commercial traffic, and far less turbulence. Atlas's pressurization, materials, and life-support systems are certified to FAA Part 25 amendments specifically written for stratospheric operation.

What's the deposit refund policy?

All deposits are fully refundable at any time before your first booked flight. We hold them in a regulated escrow account.

How loud is takeoff for surrounding communities?

Atlas takes off using its electric powertrain alone, then transitions to hybrid cruise above 25,000 feet. Ground-level noise is roughly equivalent to highway traffic at 50 meters — about 58 dB.

Will Atlas fly to my city?

We're launching with twelve cities and adding routes quarterly. Reservation holders vote on the next destination each quarter.
